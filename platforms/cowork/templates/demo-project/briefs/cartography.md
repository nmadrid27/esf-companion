# Critical Cartography Series

## Project description

A series of five maps that foreground absent Indigenous place names in a defined region of the Pacific Northwest. The maps are not illustrations of erasure; they are arguments about it. Each map names what has been removed, by name, and treats the act of naming as the primary visual gesture of the work.

## Deliverables

- Five maps, printed at 24" x 36", on archival paper.
- Wall text for the series (one paragraph, ~150 words).
- Captions for each map (one to three sentences each).
- A short artist statement (300 to 500 words) for the exhibition catalog.

## Constraints

- All source data must be public domain or under licenses that explicitly permit critical recontextualization.
- Indigenous place names must be sourced from communities with documented consent, not extracted from public databases without provenance.
- The series must function as a single argument, not five independent objects.

## Critique date

Two weeks from project start. Final crit is the only formal review point.

## Audience

Studio crit panel (faculty and peers), and the gallery audience for the spring exhibition. The crit panel will read the artist statement; the gallery audience will read the wall text.
