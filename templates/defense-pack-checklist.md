# Defense Pack Manual Assembly

For Path 1 users (templates-only, no Claude Code install). The Defense Pack render is automated in Path 4 (Claude Code); on Path 1 you assemble by hand. The result is the same artifact: a coherent, defensible packet you walk an instructor through.

---

## What you'll produce

A folder containing:
- Your Position Statement
- Your Records of Resistance (selected key ones + the full set)
- Your AI Use Log (if you kept one)
- Your Reflection
- A new `defense-narrative.md` (you write this; template provided)
- A short Disclosure Statement

You can zip the folder, email it, drop it into Canvas, or share it however your course expects.

---

## Checklist

1. **Gather your existing files** into a single folder named `defense-pack/`:
   - `position-statement.md`
   - All `record-of-resistance-*.md` files
   - `ai-use-log.md` (if present)
   - `reflection.md` (if present)
   - `disclosure-statement.md` (if present)

2. **Open `templates/defense-narrative-template.md`** and fill it in:
   - Copy your stance into "How I came in"
   - Copy what matters most + non-negotiables into "What I set out to protect"
   - **Pick 3 to 5 Records of Resistance** that most directly defend your stance. For each, write a one-paragraph narration in your own voice: what AI suggested, why you overruled it, what you did instead, and how that connects to your Position Statement.
   - Write your drift summary into "How my position held"
   - Restate your Five Questions answers as defensible claims under "What I'd defend if asked"
   - Paste or rewrite your disclosure under "Disclosure"

3. **Save** the filled narrative as `defense-pack/defense-narrative.md`.

4. **Practice the walkthrough.** Read your narrative aloud once before the defense. Aim for 12 to 15 minutes.

5. **Five Questions self-check** (from `templates/five-questions-checklist.md`):
   - Can I defend this without saying "the AI suggested it"?
   - Is this mine?
   - Did I verify?
   - Would I teach this?
   - Is my disclosure honest?

6. **Share the folder** with your instructor in the format your course expects.

---

*This checklist exists because the Defense Pack should work even without install. The Path 4 (Claude Code) version automates steps 1, 2, and 4 of this checklist.*
